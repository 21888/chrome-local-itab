# 1.1.61 · 安全另存提示词 / Safely save prompt copies

## 新增

- 已有提示词编辑器提供“将草稿保存为新提示词”，复制原始模板与编辑元数据，创建新身份及一个正文版本，保留原记录和历史。
- 包括普通编辑、冲突与历史上限恢复场景；普通保存和快捷键仍编辑原提示词。

## 修复

- 不确定确认结果保留同一不可变操作；后续冲突不能被误判为先前写入失败，也不会解锁另一次添加。
- “检查保存结果”只读比对精确操作 ID 与命令摘要回执；找到证明时显示当前保存状态，不重写记录或再发新操作。

## 注意事项

- 缺失、被淘汰或不匹配的回执不证明失败，草稿与操作继续保留在当前标签页；请保持页面并检查资料库。
- 不放宽原变更重放与并发校验，不自动回滚、按标题猜测或创建额外恢复副本；操作日志不是跨重载持久化日志。
- 填写变量与历史预览选择不写入复制模板。合成测试不替代原生存储和完整 CI 验收。

- 本地独立检查通过 2,065 项，Python 检查通过 53 项。20,000 链接用例未在本地重跑，完整本地验证仍不完整；发布仍须对应提交的原有完整 CI 通过，未放宽断言。
- 独立浏览器页面以 9 个与生产逐字节匹配的资产和显式内存故障控制验证普通复制保留字面占位符、创建一个新版本，并排除手动填写的预览值。丢失确认并插入后续写入后，只读检查证明原复制已提交，写入保持 4 次、记录 3 个；移除回执后连续两次检查仍不新增写入，保留草稿并阻止新保存。
- 这是英文桌面合成内存验收；原历史保持由计数与列表正文支持，未单独重开历史。夹具预填预览未出现，改由普通界面填写。真实持久化、多进程竞争、中文、深色/窄屏、完整键盘焦点与逐字段标签/分类复制未独立验证。

## English

- Adds Save draft as new prompt to existing-record editors, including ordinary editing and conflict/history-limit recovery. It copies exact template/metadata into a fresh record with one body version, leaving the original/history intact.
- Normal Save and its keyboard shortcut still update the original; filled variables and historical preview choices are never substituted into a copy.
- Uncertain acknowledgements retain an immutable operation. An intervening conflict does not establish earlier failure or unlock another add. Check saved outcome uses exact operation ID/command-digest receipt proof under a read-only lock, without rewriting records or creating a new command.
- Missing, evicted or mismatched receipts remain uncertain. Keep the tab open and inspect the library; no speculative rollback or recovery copy occurs. Existing replay/CAS checks remain strict, and pending operation state is not a durable journal.
- Synthetic checks do not replace native storage and complete CI acceptance.

- Local checks passed 2,065 unique cases plus 53 Python checks. The 20,000-link case was not rerun locally, leaving local validation incomplete. Publication requires the exact commit’s existing complete CI gate; no assertions were relaxed.
- Native browser interaction on an isolated page with nine byte-identical shipped assets and explicit in-memory fault controls verified an ordinary copy keeps literal placeholders, creates one version and excludes manually filled preview values. After a committed lost acknowledgment and intervening write, read-only receipt proof resolved the original copy with writes fixed at four and records at three. Removing the receipt left two checks read-only, retained the draft and blocked fresh saves.
- This is English desktop synthetic-memory evidence. Original-history preservation is supported by its counter and unchanged list body, rather than separately reopening history. An absent fixture-prefilled preview was filled through ordinary UI. Actual durability, multi-process races, Chinese, dark/narrow presentation, full keyboard/focus coverage and field-by-field tag/category copying were not independently verified.
