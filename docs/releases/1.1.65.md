# 1.1.65 · 准确显示优化稿保存状态 / Accurate refinement save labels

## 新增

- 优化稿标题与文本框辅助名称以中英文区分可编辑、只读、确认中、未确认锁定和已保存状态。

## 修复

- 明确采纳或只读核对确认成功后，不再错误显示“可编辑/未保存”。
- 未确认、冲突或过期来源不会误称已保存；试跑结束后的可编辑状态同步恢复。

## 注意事项

- 仅改变状态措辞与辅助名称，不修改保存、请求、权限或历史行为。

- 本地独立检查通过 2,110 项，Python 检查通过 53 项。20,000 链接用例未在本地重跑，完整本地验证仍不完整；发布须对应提交的原有完整 CI 通过。
- 独立浏览器页面使用 18 个生产匹配资产，分别在英文浅色与中文深色中验证采纳后正确显示已保存只读标题、输入尝试不改变文本；各一次合成请求、两次写入（含种子）、两个历史版本。
- 辅助名称未能通过原生 AX 独立读取，相关一致性仅有自动化 DOM 证据。语言/主题由夹具提供，不代表扩展偏好持久化；真实提供商、授权、存储持久化、窄屏与完整键盘未验证。

## English

- Refinement headings and textarea accessible names distinguish editable, read-only, confirming, unconfirmed/locked and saved states in English and Chinese.
- Successful acceptance or confirmed read-only outcome no longer says editable/not saved. Uncertain saves, conflicts and stale sources do not falsely claim success, and trial closure restores the appropriate editable label.
- This changes status wording and accessible names only, preserving save, request, permission and history behavior.

- Local checks passed 2,110 unique cases plus 53 Python checks. The 20,000-link case was not rerun locally, leaving local validation incomplete. Publication requires the exact commit’s existing complete CI gate.
- Native interaction using 18 byte-identical assets independently verified English/light and Chinese/dark saved headings and read-only behavior after acceptance. Each synthetic session ended with one request, two writes including its seed, and two history versions.
- Native AX did not expose textarea accessible names; their equality remains automated DOM evidence only. Fixture language/theme selection does not establish extension preference persistence. Real providers, grants, durable storage, narrow layout and full keyboard coverage remain unverified.
