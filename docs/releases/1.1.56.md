# 1.1.56 · 原模板与建议试跑 / Original-versus-proposal trials

## 新增

- 收到有效优化建议后，可临时对比原已保存模板与未接受的建议，使用同一组新测试变量和同一模型。
- 发送前分别审核两份完整提示词、目标地址与模型，并提示最多额外两次请求和可能费用。

## 修复

- 测试值、来源、建议或连接变化使旧审核失效；独立失败、取消与迟到响应不会混淆两份答案。
- 试跑不自动接受建议、修改历史或保存回答；关闭清空测试值与答案，返回原建议。

## 注意事项

- 不复制之前已填预览值。测试密钥临时共用于这两次请求，不持久化。
- 仅在已有有效建议后可用；接受为新版本仍为独立操作。试跑回答不能保存为已保存模板版本结果。
- 自动测试使用合成响应，没有真实授权、凭据或提供商请求；原生与实际提供商验收单独说明。

- 本地独立测试通过 2,009 项，Python 检查通过 53 项；20,000 链接规模用例达到资源时间上限，完整本地测试仍未完成。该用例及十个依赖文件与已验证 1.1.55 相同，未削弱断言或跳过现有 CI；发布必须以对应提交的完整 CI 通过为前提。
- 原生浏览器在显著标注的独立测试页面中，使用与生产一致的 UI/控制器/客户端解析器及合成传输、模拟权限、内存存储，验证同值双答案、无试跑保存、取消与独立接受。两次建议请求加两次试跑共四次模拟请求；初始创建后写入保持一次，显式接受后增至两次且历史为两版。测试资产未进入生产 ZIP。
- 这不验证真实提供商、Chrome 权限、扩展持久化或真实模型质量。已接受后的建议标题可能仍显示“未保存”，但状态、按钮禁用与历史正确。

## English

- After a valid refinement proposal arrives, optionally trial the original saved template and unsaved proposal with one fresh variable mapping and the same endpoint/model.
- Reviews both full prompts and destination before up to two additional requests, with separate potential costs disclosed. Earlier filled-preview values are not copied.
- Changes invalidate review; failures, Stop and late responses remain fenced. A temporary key is shared only for the two requests and is not persisted.
- Trials never accept a proposal, alter history or save answers. Closing clears trial values/results and returns to the proposal. Accepting a new version remains separate.
- Automated tests use synthetic responses, without real credentials, grants or provider calls. Native and live-provider acceptance are separate.

- Local checks passed 2,009 unique cases plus 53 Python checks. The 20,000-link scale case hit its resource timeout, so complete local validation remains unfinished. Its test and ten dependencies match verified 1.1.55; assertions and the existing full CI are unchanged. Publication requires the exact commit’s complete CI gate to pass.
- Native browser interaction on a visibly labeled isolated test page used byte-identical shipped UI/controllers/client parser with fake transport/permissions and in-memory stores. It verified same-value paired answers, no trial-save controls, cancellation and separate acceptance: four simulated requests (two proposals plus two trial calls), writes remaining one until explicit acceptance then two, and exactly two history versions. Test assets are excluded from the production ZIP.
- This does not verify real providers, Chrome grants, durable extension storage or model quality. The proposal heading can retain “not saved” after acceptance; status, disabled acceptance and saved history correctly reflect completion.
